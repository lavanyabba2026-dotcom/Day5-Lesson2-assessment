# Lesson 2: Your Own Task, Walked and Improved

## Easy

### 1. Why should a student turn the seven task-path answers into one clear message?

A student should turn the seven task-path answers into one clear message because it helps the AI understand the purpose, relevant facts, task, and expected result. A clear message reduces confusion and makes it easier for the AI to provide a useful and relevant answer.

### 2. What does it mean to find one weak step in a first attempt?

Finding one weak step means identifying the part of the task process that caused the result to be unclear, incomplete, or incorrect. Instead of starting over, the student should improve that specific step by adding missing information or making the request clearer.

### 3. Why is "it looks fine" not enough before trusting an AI answer?

"It looks fine" is not enough because an answer can appear correct while still containing mistakes or missing important details. A student should check the result against the original goal, verify important facts, and make sure it meets their actual needs before trusting it.

## Intermediate

### 4. A student asks, "Help me with my assignment." The result is too general. Which task-path step is weak? What should the student add?

The task step is weak because the student has not clearly explained what kind of help they need. They should provide the assignment topic, relevant instructions, important details, and the specific output they expect, such as an explanation, an outline, or answers to particular questions. This will help the AI give a more focused and useful response.

### 5. A student gets a revision timetable but never checks whether the daily workload fits their real pace. What human step did they skip?

The student skipped the checking step. They should verify whether the timetable matches the actual number of days remaining and whether the daily workload is realistic for their learning pace and other commitments. They should also make their own adjustments before following the plan.

## Hard

### 6. Choose one real task you need this week. Write a clear message for an AI tool using purpose, useful facts, task, and expected result.

**My task:** Preparing a revision timetable for an upcoming exam.

**Message to the AI:**

"I have an upcoming exam and need help preparing a revision timetable. My subject is [subject name], and the topics I need to study are [list of topics]. I have [number of days] days left before my exam. Please create a realistic, day-by-day revision timetable that covers all the topics and suggests how much time I should spend on each one. Present the timetable in a simple, organised format that is easy to follow. Make sure the workload is manageable and leave some time for revision and practice questions."

### 7. Your first result is off-target. Name one weak step you would fix, what you would change, and what you would check in the new result.

One weak step I would fix is providing useful facts. If the first timetable does not meet my needs, I would give the AI more specific information about my topics, the days remaining, and the time I can study each day.

After improving the request, I would check whether the new timetable covers all the required topics, matches the actual number of days remaining, and has a realistic daily workload. I would also adjust the plan according to my own pace before following it.
